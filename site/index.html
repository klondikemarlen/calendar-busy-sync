Your calendars. A local connection.

Only Busy copies

Copies contain the time interval, Busy status, private ownership metadata, and a fixed origin note. No source titles, descriptions, locations, guests, or meeting links are copied.

You choose the direction

Google → Outlook is the default and uses read-only Google access. Optional reciprocal mode also writes Busy copies to Google. Original events are not edited.

No hosted sync service

The Python command-line tool connects directly to Google and Microsoft from your computer. Credentials and sync references stay in private local files, not on a Calendar Busy Sync server.

Install, then connect your accounts

Ubuntu 26.04 packages are available from the project’s public PPA. There is no subscription charge for Calendar Busy Sync.

  1. Install the CLI. Follow the PPA installation instructions, or use the published release artifacts.
  2. Configure and authorize. Run calendar-busy-sync setup. You currently supply your own Google Desktop OAuth client and Microsoft application registration; guided registration assistance is available. Review the privacy policy before granting access.
  3. Sync, then optionally schedule. Follow the setup and operation guide. Installation alone never authorizes accounts, syncs calendars, or enables a scheduled job.

Keep the computer awake and online for scheduled sync. Google consent and Microsoft tenant policies still apply. Busy status does not prevent a conflicting invitation. Coworker Scheduling Assistant visibility remains independently unverified; see the remaining live check.

Open source, with a clear point of contact

Questions or privacy requests: calendar-busy-sync-support@googlegroups.com. Report bugs on GitHub; never include tokens, credentials, or real calendar details in public reports.
